## 2026-10-28 - [Edit Toggle for Workout Sets]
**Learning:** Preventing frustrating typo lock-ins within active sessions is essential. For forms with many repeated items like workout sets, it's better UX to provide an undo/edit toggle rather than permanently locking the row after submission.
**Action:** Always provide an 'undo' or edit toggle for submitted data states within an active session rather than permanently locking the row.
