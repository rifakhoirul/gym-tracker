## 2024-05-15 - [UX Directive: Prevent Typos from Being Locked In]
**Learning:** Currently, once a user marks a workout set as "Done" during an active session, the row gets permanently locked (inputs become non-editable, and the button becomes disabled). This is frustrating if the user makes a typo in weight/reps and accidentally submits it, with no way to fix it.
**Action:** Always provide an 'undo' or edit toggle for submitted data states within an active session (e.g., workout sets), rather than permanently locking the row, to prevent frustrating typo lock-ins.
