## 2026-10-29 - [Undo Action for Completed Sets]
**Learning:** When users complete a task or form (like logging a workout set), permanently locking the row can be frustrating and lead to typo lock-ins if they made a mistake. Providing a simple "undo" action improves the user experience by giving them control to fix errors instantly.
**Action:** Always provide an 'undo' or edit toggle for submitted data states within an active session, rather than permanently locking the row.
