## 2023-10-24 - Undo Toggle for Set Completion
**Learning:** Users can make typos when entering reps and weights during a workout. Locking the row permanently after completion can cause frustration, as they cannot correct their mistakes without deleting and re-adding the set.
**Action:** Always provide an "undo" or edit toggle for submitted data states within an active session (e.g., workout sets) rather than permanently locking the row, to prevent frustrating typo lock-ins.
